01 — The problem

India's scrap trade works. It just works in the dark.

India generates over 62 million tonnes of municipal waste a year, and the informal sector recovers the majority of what actually gets recycled. Yet neither side of that trade has price visibility. (Hover a card for detail.)

    09 — Roadmap & model

    How it scales, and how it earns.

    Revenue 01

    Transaction fee

    A small platform fee per completed pickup, charged to the aggregation margin, never to the household.

    Revenue 02

    Aggregation spread

    Bulk sorted volume sells to recyclers at materially better rates than fragmented informal supply.

    Revenue 03

    EPR & marketplace

    Verified recovery data for brands' EPR obligations, plus a commission on Kalakriti marketplace sales.
